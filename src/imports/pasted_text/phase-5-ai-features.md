Upgrade the existing Vault Ventures project with the Phase 5 AI Business Analysis, Investor Readiness, Matching, and Recommendation experience.

IMPORTANT:
Use the existing Vault Ventures project as the baseline.
Preserve all existing screens, layouts, navigation, components, and working functionality.
Do not rebuild the application.
Do not redesign unrelated features.

GOAL:
Add the UI/UX for the following Phase 5 capabilities:
- AI Business Analysis
- Investor Readiness Score
- Explainable improvement suggestions
- AI Matching Engine
- Match Score
- Match explanations
- AI Recommendations

1. AI BUSINESS ANALYSIS

For a published business, provide an AI Business Analysis section.

It should communicate:
- Overall business assessment
- Key strengths
- Areas that need improvement
- Relevant business insights
- Clear explanations for the assessment

Keep the presentation professional and concise.

Do not present AI output as guaranteed financial advice.

2. INVESTOR READINESS SCORE

Add a clearly defined Readiness Score experience for businesses.

Show:
- Overall score
- Score visualization
- Key contributing factors
- Strengths
- Improvement areas

The score must feel explainable rather than a mysterious number.

Do not invent unsupported scoring formulas in the UI.

3. AI IMPROVEMENT SUGGESTIONS

Provide actionable suggestions based on the business analysis/readiness assessment.

Examples of presentation:
- Improve business description
- Clarify market opportunity
- Strengthen financial information
- Improve team/skill coverage

Only display suggestions supported by the available business information.

Do not create random or fake AI results.

4. AI MATCHING ENGINE

Create the UI for matching users with relevant opportunities/people.

Support appropriate matching between:
- Businesses ↔ Investors
- Businesses ↔ Professionals

Show:
- Match Score
- Match reasons
- Relevant shared attributes
- Strengths of the match
- Potential gaps

5. MATCH SCORE

Display Match Score clearly but professionally.

Example:
94% Match

The UI must also explain WHY the match exists.

Never show only a percentage without explanation.

6. MATCH EXPLANATION

Include explainable factors such as:
- Industry alignment
- Investment preference alignment
- Skill alignment
- Experience alignment
- Business-stage alignment
- Other supported matching attributes

Do not invent unsupported factors.

7. RECOMMENDATIONS

Create an AI Recommendations experience for users.

Recommendations may include:
- Relevant businesses
- Relevant investors
- Relevant professionals

Each recommendation should provide a concise explanation of why it is relevant.

Do not present recommendations as guaranteed or authoritative decisions.

8. AI VISUAL LANGUAGE

This is Phase 5, so Copper MAY be used to distinguish AI-originated content.

However:
- Do not turn the entire interface into an AI-themed UI.
- Keep the core Vault Ventures identity Charcoal + Copper.
- AI indicators should be subtle and professional.
- Avoid excessive glowing effects.
- Avoid futuristic/casino-style visuals.
- Keep AI sections clearly distinguishable from normal deterministic Discovery.

9. DISCOVERY SEPARATION

The existing Phase 4 Search/Filter/Discovery experience must remain deterministic.

Do not replace normal search/filter results with AI ranking.

Clearly distinguish:
- User-controlled Discovery
from
- AI-powered Recommendations/Matching

10. LOADING / EMPTY / ERROR STATES

Add appropriate states for AI features:
- Analysis loading
- Analysis unavailable
- No recommendation available
- Matching unavailable
- Error generating analysis
- Data insufficient for analysis

Do not display fake scores while loading.

11. EXPLAINABILITY

Whenever showing:
- Readiness Score
- Match Score
- AI Recommendation

provide a way for the user to understand the major reasons behind the result.

Avoid black-box presentation.

12. STRICT DATA HONESTY

Do NOT:
- Invent AI models
- Invent machine-learning claims
- Use random scores
- Use fake AI results
- Hardcode results as if they were generated dynamically
- Claim deep learning/ML infrastructure that does not exist
- Present simulated AI output as real production intelligence

The UI must be designed so actual deterministic/explainable AI logic can later populate the content.

13. DO NOT IMPLEMENT LATER PHASES

Do not implement:
- Staged Disclosure
- NDA
- Secure document access
- Interest/Application
- Connections
- Deal Room
- Negotiation
- Agreement
- Investment execution
- Reputation
- Admin governance

14. PRESERVE EXISTING FEATURES

Do not remove or replace:
- Search
- Filters
- Discovery
- Profiles
- Business management
- Verification

Integrate the AI experiences naturally into the existing product.

STRICT SCOPE:

ONLY implement the Phase 5 UI/UX for:
AI Business Analysis
Readiness Score
AI Improvement Suggestions
AI Matching
Match Score
Match Explanation
AI Recommendations

FINAL RESULT:

Vault Ventures should clearly separate:
1. Deterministic Search & Discovery
2. Explainable AI Analysis & Recommendations

The AI experience must feel premium, trustworthy, explainable, and consistent with the existing Charcoal + Copper product identity.
