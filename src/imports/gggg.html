How this round stacks against round 1

None of these four beat direction A (obsidian + champagne gold) on the combination of premium, trust and durability — A is still the strongest overall pick. But two are worth keeping on the shortlist: G, charcoal + copper, is a genuinely strong alternate to A if you want something slightly cooler and more industrial than gold — copper reads as "engineering precision meets capital" rather than "old money." And J, ink + aurora, is the most distinctive gradient of everything shown across both rounds — a real option if Vault Ventures wants a signature, ownable visual moment (e.g. on the landing page hero or match-score reveal) even if the rest of the product stays closer to A or G for day-to-day surfaces.
